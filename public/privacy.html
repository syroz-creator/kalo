Privacy

Updated October 20, 2026

Nutrition results are estimates, not measurements or medical advice. Gemini-powered estimates are for adults aged 18 and over.

What Stays on Your Device

Your profile, goals, meal history, saved recipes, water, workouts and body progress photos are stored in your browser or app on this device. There is no automatic account sync. Clearing browser data or deleting the app can remove those records.

Meal Estimates

When you request an estimate, Kalo sends the selected meal photo or description, portion notes and output language directly from your device to Google Gemini using your own API key. Your history, profile and body progress photos are not included. Meal estimates do not pass through Kalo's server.

Your API key stays in this device's local app or browser storage. It is sent to Google's API for requests and is excluded from Kalo backups. Removing the key in Settings does not delete your meals. Local storage is not a dedicated encrypted credential vault; use only your own key on a trusted device.

Google's data handling depends on your project's applicable service terms. Unpaid services may use submitted content and responses for product improvement and human review. Paid-service terms exclude that use but permit limited safety retention. Do not submit faces, documents or other personal, sensitive or confidential content. See Google's Gemini API terms before using estimates.

Backups and Other Services

Optional Kalo Pro subscriptions on iPhone are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives an app-specific customer identifier and purchase/subscription information to manage Pro access. Payment card details are not sent to Kalo. Kalo does not send your meal photos, meal history, body progress photos or Gemini API key to RevenueCat. Meal suggestions use the current meal and reference food portions on your device, without uploading another photo. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription.

Backups include personal records and photos. Keep downloaded backups private. Restoring a backup is a manual action; Kalo does not upload it for cloud storage.

Product and food searches may send the product barcode or search text to Open Food Facts. Contact messages send your message and optional reply email through Kalo's server and Resend to the operator. No advertising trackers or general-purpose behavioral analytics are added by Kalo.

Your Choices

Manual logging does not require Gemini. Camera and photo selection are optional. You can remove your saved API key in Settings, export a backup, delete individual meals and progress photos, or clear meal and workout history. Those history controls do not delete every category of local data; deleting all app or browser storage removes the rest. Export first if you want to keep a copy.
